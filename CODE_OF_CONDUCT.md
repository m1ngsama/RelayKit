# Code Of Conduct

RelayKit is a remote access project. Discussions can involve sensitive safety
and abuse concerns, so contributors are expected to keep collaboration direct,
respectful, and evidence-based.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent, but require concrete evidence for safety claims.
- Keep criticism focused on the code, design, documentation, or threat model.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private relay URLs, active session codes,
  credentials, user-identifying logs, or exploit details in public threads.
- Prefer responsible disclosure for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing active exploit instructions for unauthorized access.
- Encouraging stealth installation, credential theft, persistence without
  consent, or security-control bypass.
- Repeatedly derailing security reviews or ignoring maintainer boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or
restrict participation when behavior harms project safety or collaboration.

Security reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.
